Reading this capture

Protected frames carry encrypted content; this report does not decrypt them. A retry is a retransmitted frame, not necessarily a capture error.

Device counts represent observed MAC addresses. An address pair shows captured traffic, not proof of an association. Zero associated clients means none were recorded.
